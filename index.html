National Petroleum Distribution Policy

The KyanliGas State Authority (KyanliGas) supervises the distribution and pricing of petroleum products throughout the Republic. As the country's sole officially designated gasoline station, it maintains a unified national price schedule to ensure consistent and orderly access to fuel. All prices are established by the State and are equally applied to consumers.
